# Platform Rules

## Prohibited Items

The following items **may not** be listed, sold, or purchased on JoyMini:

### Illegal Items
- Drugs, narcotics, and drug paraphernalia
- Firearms, weapons, and ammunition
- Stolen goods
- Counterfeit currency and stamps
- Endangered species and wildlife products

### Restricted Items
- Alcohol and tobacco products (requires special license)
- Prescription medications
- Fireworks and explosives
- Nitrate products (regulated by PNP)
- Radioactive materials

### Prohibited Digital Goods
- Hacking tools and services
- Fake documents and IDs
- Account hacking services
- Copyright-infringing content (pirated movies, software)
- Gambling-related services

### Other Prohibited Items
- Human remains or body parts
- Obscene or pornographic materials
- Items promoting discrimination or hate
- Government-issued IDs and documents
- Lottery tickets and sweepstakes

> **Note**: This list is not exhaustive. JoyMini reserves the right to remove any listing that violates our policies.

## Account Suspension Policy

### Grounds for Suspension

| Violation | First Offense | Second Offense | Third Offense |
|:----------|:-------------:|:--------------:|:-------------:|
| Selling prohibited items | Warning + delist | 7-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| Abusing coupon system | Warning | 3-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| Fake reviews / ratings | Warning | 7-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| Harassment of staff | Warning | 3-day suspension | Permanent ban |
| Fraudulent chargebacks | Account suspension | Permanent ban | — |
| Multiple accounts (fraud) | 30-day suspension | Permanent ban | — |
| Identity theft | Permanent ban | — | — |

### Suspension Process

1. **Notification**: You will receive an email and in-app notification explaining the violation.
2. **Appeal period**: You have **7 days** to submit an appeal.
3. **Review**: Our team reviews the appeal within **3-5 business days**.
4. **Decision**: If approved, the account is reinstated. If denied, the suspension remains.

### During Suspension

- **You cannot** place new orders.
- **You cannot** withdraw wallet funds (frozen).
- **Existing orders** will still be delivered (if already shipped).
- **Pending refunds** will still be processed.

## How to Appeal a Suspension

1. Check your email for the suspension notice (includes violation details).
2. Go to **Account → Appeal Center**.
3. Submit your appeal with:
   - Explanation of the situation
   - Any supporting evidence
   - Acknowledgment of the platform rules
4. Wait for the review result (3-5 business days).
5. You can re-appeal if the first appeal is denied (within 14 days).

## Reporting Violations

If you see a listing or user that violates these rules:

1. Go to the **product page** or **user profile**.
2. Tap the **Report** button (flag icon).
3. Select the violation type.
4. Add any additional information or evidence.
5. Submit the report. Reports are anonymous.

## Dispute Resolution

If you have a dispute with another user (seller/buyer):

| Issue | Resolution Process |
|:------|:-------------------|
| Item not as described | File a dispute via **Order → Report Issue** within 48 hours |
| Non-delivery | File a dispute after the estimated delivery date + 3 days |
| Quality dispute | Contact support with photos and order details |
| Refund dispute | If seller refuses refund, escalate to JoyMini mediation |

See [Dispute Resolution](contact-support.en.md) for the full process.

## Policy Updates

- JoyMini may update these rules at any time.
- Users will be notified of significant changes via email and in-app notification.
- Continued use of the platform after changes means acceptance of the new rules.
