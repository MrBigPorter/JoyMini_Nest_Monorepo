# Privacy Policy

## Information We Collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|:---------|:---------|:--------|
| **Account Info** | Name, email, phone number, address | Account registration, order delivery |
| **Identity Documents** | Passport, UMID, Driver's License | KYC verification (required by Philippine regulations) |
| **Payment Info** | GCash number, card details (tokenized) | Payment processing |
| **Device Info** | IP address, device type, browser | Security, fraud prevention |
| **Usage Data** | Pages visited, search history, purchases | Service improvement, recommendations |
| **Location** | Shipping address, IP geolocation | Delivery, regional pricing |

## How We Use Your Data

- **Order fulfillment**: Process and deliver your orders.
- **Verification**: KYC checks as required by Philippine anti-money laundering laws.
- **Customer support**: Respond to inquiries and resolve issues.
- **Personalization**: Recommend products and offers tailored to you.
- **Fraud prevention**: Detect and prevent fraudulent transactions.
- **Legal compliance**: Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## Data Sharing

We **do not sell** your personal information. We may share data with:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|:----------|:--------|:------------|
| Payment processors (GCash, PayMaya, banks) | Payment processing | Payment details (tokenized) |
| Logistics partners (J&T, LBC, Ninja Van, 2GO) | Order delivery | Name, address, phone |
| KYC verification providers | Identity verification | ID documents |
| Law enforcement | Legal compliance | As required by law |

## Your Rights

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|:------|:------------|:----------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of your data | Account → Settings → Download My Data |
| **Correction** | Correct inaccurate data | Edit profile or contact support |
| **Deletion** | Request account deletion | Account → Deactivate Account |
| **Objection** | Opt out of marketing | Unsubscribe in email settings |
| **Portability** | Transfer your data | Contact support |

## Data Retention

- **Account data**: Retained until account deletion.
- **KYC documents**: Retained for **5 years** after account closure (regulatory requirement).
- **Transaction records**: Retained for **10 years** (tax and audit purposes).
- **Marketing preferences**: Retained until opt-out.

## Cookies

We use cookies for:

- **Essential**: Login sessions, cart management, security.
- **Analytics**: Page views, user behavior (Google Analytics).
- **Personalization**: Saved preferences, product recommendations.
- **Marketing**: Targeted advertisements (opt-in required).

You can manage cookie preferences in **Account → Privacy Settings**.

## Data Security

- All data transmitted over **TLS 1.3 encryption**.
- Payment data is **PCI-DSS compliant** and tokenized.
- KYC documents stored in **encrypted storage** with access logging.
- Regular security audits and penetration testing.
