# Promotion & Sale Events

## Types of Promotions

JoyMini runs several types of promotions throughout the year:

| Promotion Type | Description | Frequency |
|:---------------|:------------|:----------|
| **Daily Flash Sale** | Limited-time discounts on selected items | Daily |
| **Weekly Deals** | Special prices on curated products | Weekly |
| **Monthly Mega Sale** | Site-wide discounts across categories | Monthly |
| **Holiday Sale** | Seasonal promotions (Christmas, New Year, etc.) | Per holiday |
| **11.11 / Double Day** | Major sales event on 11.11, 12.12, etc. | Annually |
| **Anniversary Sale** | JoyMini foundation anniversary event | Annually |
| **Brand Days** | Specific brand promotions | Per schedule |
| **Referral Bonus** | Discounts for referring new users | Ongoing |

## How Promotions Work

### Discount Types

| Discount Type | How It Works | Example |
|:--------------|:-------------|:--------|
| **Percentage off** | Discount = price × percentage | 50% off ₱1,000 = pay ₱500 |
| **Fixed amount off** | Flat discount on qualifying orders | ₱100 off orders over ₱500 |
| **Buy X Get Y** | Free or discounted additional item | Buy 1 Get 1 50% off |
| **Free shipping** | Shipping fee waived | Free shipping on orders over ₱500 |
| **Bundle deal** | Discounted price when buying multiple items | Bundle of 3 for ₱999 |

### Eligibility

- Most promotions are **open to all users** with a verified account.
- Some promotions are **tier-restricted** (e.g., Platinum & Diamond members only).
- Flash Sale items may have **limited stock** — first come, first served.
- Promotions **cannot be combined** unless explicitly stated.

## Important Rules

### Stacking Rules

| Scenario | Can Stack? |
|:---------|:----------:|
| Coupon + Promotional price | ❌ Usually not |
| Multiple coupons | ❌ Never |
| Flash Sale + Coupon | ❌ Never |
| Free Shipping + Other discount | ✅ Yes |
| Cashback + Promotional price | ✅ Yes |

### During a Promotion

- Prices shown are **final** — no additional negotiation.
- Promotional inventory is **limited** — once sold out, the promotion ends.
- Promotional items may have **different return policies** (see product page).
- JoyMini reserves the right to **modify or cancel** promotions at any time.

### After a Promotion Ends

- Items return to **regular pricing**.
- Unused promotional coupons **expire**.
- Flash Sale items still in cart will be **updated to regular price** at checkout.

## Common Questions

### "How do I know about upcoming promotions?"

| Channel | How to Stay Informed |
|:--------|:---------------------|
| **In-app notifications** | Enable push notifications in Settings |
| **Email** | Subscribe to marketing emails |
| **Home page banner** | Check the JoyMini home page regularly |
| **Social media** | Follow JoyMini on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok |
| **SMS alerts** | Opt in for SMS promotions (standard rates apply) |

### "I missed a Flash Sale — will it come back?"

- Some Flash Sales are **recurring** (e.g., Daily Flash Sale).
- Special event Flash Sales (11.11, Anniversary) are **annual** — wait for next year.
- Tap **"Notify Me"** on sold-out items to get alerts for the next sale.

### "Can I return a promotional item?"

| Item Type | Return Policy |
|:----------|:--------------|
| Regular items bought at discount | Standard 7-day return policy applies |
| Flash Sale items | **Non-returnable** (unless damaged) |
| Lucky Draw items | **Non-returnable** (unless damaged) |
| Bundle deals | Must return **all items** in the bundle |

See [Refund Policy](../order/refund-policy.en.md) for full details.

### "I bought an item and now it's on sale — can I get a price adjustment?"

We do **not** offer price adjustments or retroactive discounts. However:
- You can **cancel and re-order** if the original order hasn't shipped yet.
- Some promotions offer **price protection** for a limited window (check promotion terms).

### "Why didn't I receive the promotional coupon?"

Possible reasons:
1. The promotion was **region-specific** (Metro Manila only, for example).
2. You needed to **claim the coupon first** during the promotion period.
3. The promotion had a **limited number of coupons** (first-come-first-served).
4. Your **account tier** may not qualify (some promotions are VIP-only).

### "Can I use my voucher during a promotion?"

Most promotion prices **cannot be combined** with vouchers/coupons. The system will automatically apply the **better deal** (either the promotion price or your coupon, whichever gives the bigger discount).

## Terms & Conditions

- JoyMini reserves the right to **modify, suspend, or cancel** any promotion at any time without prior notice.
- Promotions are **non-transferable** and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- If fraudulent activity is detected, JoyMini may **revoke promotional benefits** and take appropriate action.
- In case of pricing errors, JoyMini reserves the right to **cancel affected orders**.
