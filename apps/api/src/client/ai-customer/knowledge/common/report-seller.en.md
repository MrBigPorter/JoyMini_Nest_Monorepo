# Report a Seller or Listing

## What Can Be Reported

### Seller Violations

| Violation Type | Examples |
|:---------------|:---------|
| **Fake / counterfeit products** | Selling unauthorized replicas, fake branded items |
| **Prohibited items** | Weapons, drugs, illegal goods |
| **Misleading listings** | Wrong product photos, false descriptions |
| **Price gouging** | Unreasonably high prices during emergencies |
| **Harassment** | Abusive messages, threats, spam |
| **Fraud** | Fake tracking numbers, not shipping orders |
| **Multiple accounts** | Operating multiple accounts to manipulate the system |
| **Review manipulation** | Fake reviews, paying for positive reviews |

### Listing Violations

| Violation Type | Examples |
|:---------------|:---------|
| **Wrong category** | Item listed in an unrelated category |
| **Misleading title** | Title mentions a brand that the product is not |
| **Incomplete description** | Missing key information (size, material, etc.) |
| **Inappropriate images** | Offensive or explicit photos |
| **Copyright infringement** | Using brand images without authorization |

## How to Report

### Report a Product Listing

1. Go to the **product page**.
2. Tap the **flag icon** (⚑) or **"Report"** button near the listing.
3. Select the **violation type** from the list.
4. Add any **additional details** or evidence (optional but helpful).
5. Tap **Submit**.

### Report a Seller

1. Go to the **seller's store page**.
2. Tap the **three dots** (⋯) or **"More"** menu.
3. Select **"Report Seller"**.
4. Choose the **reason** for reporting.
5. Add details and evidence.
6. Tap **Submit**.

### Report a Message

If a seller sends inappropriate messages:
1. Open the **chat/conversation** with the seller.
2. Tap and hold the message.
3. Select **"Report"**.
4. Choose the reason.
5. Submit.

## What Happens After You Report

### Reporting Process

```
You Submit Report
       │
       ▼
System Review ──► Auto-filter (spam, obvious violations)
       │
       ▼
Manual Review (if needed) ──► 1-3 business days
       │
       ▼
Decision Made
       │
       ├── Violation confirmed → Action taken against seller/listing
       └── No violation → Report closed, no action
```

### Timeline

| Step | Timeframe |
|:-----|:----------|
| Auto-review (spam/obvious violations) | Within **1 hour** |
| Manual review (if escalated) | **1-3 business days** |
| Action taken (if violation confirmed) | Within **24 hours** of decision |
| Notification to you | After decision is made |

### Will I be notified?

Yes, you will receive an **in-app notification** with the outcome of your report:
- **"Action Taken"** — The listing/seller was found in violation and action was taken.
- **"No Action"** — The report was reviewed and no violation was found.

Note: For privacy reasons, we **cannot disclose specific actions** taken against a seller.

## Anonymous Reporting

All reports are **anonymous**. The reported seller will not know who submitted the report. Your identity is protected.

However, if you are involved in a **dispute** (e.g., you ordered and didn't receive the item), the seller may be able to infer who reported based on the context.

## False Reporting

**Knowingly submitting false reports** is a violation of platform rules:

| Offense | Consequence |
|:--------|:------------|
| First false report | Warning |
| Second false report | 7-day account restriction (cannot report) |
| Repeated false reports | Account suspension |

Please report **only genuine violations**.

## What If I'm the Seller Being Reported?

If you receive a notification that your listing or account has been reported:

1. **Review your listing** — check if it complies with [Platform Rules](platform-rules.en.md).
2. **Fix any issues** — update the listing if needed.
3. **Appeal** — if you believe the report is incorrect, submit an appeal via:
   - **Seller Center → Appeals**
   - Include evidence that your listing complies with platform rules.
4. Appeals are reviewed within **3-5 business days**.

## Escalation

### If You're Not Satisfied with the Result

1. Reply to the report notification with additional evidence.
2. Contact support and reference your **report ID**.
3. Request a **second review** by a senior team member.

### Urgent Issues

For **urgent** violations (illegal items, security threats, active fraud):
- Contact support **immediately** via in-app chat.
- Mark your message as **"Urgent"**.
- Our team prioritizes urgent reports and responds within **2 hours** during business hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Can I report anonymously?"
Yes, all reports are anonymous.

### "What if I accidentally submitted a false report?"
Contact support immediately to withdraw the report. Accidental false reports have no penalty if corrected promptly.

### "Can I report a buyer?"
Yes, sellers can report buyers for harassment, false claims, or fraud using the same process.

### "How do I know if action was taken?"
You will receive a notification. We cannot share specific details about actions taken against other accounts.

### "What if the same seller violates again after being penalized?"
Report again. Repeat violations result in **escalating penalties**, including permanent account suspension.
