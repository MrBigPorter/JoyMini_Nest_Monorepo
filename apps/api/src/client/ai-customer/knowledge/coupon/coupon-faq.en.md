# Coupon FAQ

## Common Questions

### "Why can't I use my coupon?"

| Possible Reason | Check |
|:----------------|:------|
| **Minimum spend not met** | Your order total may be below the minimum spend requirement. Add more items. |
| **Expired** | Check the validity date in My Vouchers. |
| **Item excluded** | Some items (Flash Sale, Lucky Draw) are not eligible for coupons. |
| **Already used** | Most coupons are one-time use. Check your usage history. |
| **Stacking limit** | Most coupons cannot be combined with other discounts. |
| **Account limit reached** | Some coupons are limited to first N users. |

### "My coupon code is not working"

1. **Check spelling** — codes are case-sensitive. Try copying and pasting directly.
2. **Check the format** — some codes include hyphens (e.g., JOY-10-OFF).
3. **Check if it's activated** — some codes need to be manually activated in My Vouchers.
4. **Check region** — some codes are region-specific (e.g., Metro Manila only).

### "Where do I enter my coupon code?"

On the **Checkout page**, there is a "Coupon / Promo Code" field:
1. Add items to your cart.
2. Proceed to **Checkout**.
3. Enter or paste your coupon code.
4. Tap **Apply**.
5. The discount will be shown in the order summary.

### "Applied coupon disappeared from my order"

| Possible Cause | Solution |
|:---------------|:---------|
| You changed items in the cart | Re-apply the coupon after changing items. |
| The coupon expired while you were shopping | Check the expiry date in My Vouchers. |
| You applied a different coupon | Coupons cannot be stacked; the new one replaces the old one. |
| Session expired | Your cart may have been saved but the coupon needs to be re-applied. |

### "Coupon shows in My Vouchers but can't be used"

| Reason | Solution |
|:-------|:---------|
| Targeted coupon (not for you) | Some coupons are sent to specific users only. |
| Minimum spend not met | Check the coupon's minimum spend requirement. |
| Cart is empty | Add items to your cart first. |
| Already used on another device | Coupons are synced; if used on another device, it's consumed. |

## Coupon Refund Questions

### "If I return an item, is my coupon refunded?"

| Coupon Type | Refund Policy |
|:------------|:--------------|
| **Free coupon** (given by platform) | Not refunded. The coupon is consumed. |
| **Purchased coupon** | Refunded as a new coupon with the same terms. |
| **Cashback coupon** | The cashback value stays as wallet credit. |

### "I returned a partial order, what happens to my coupon?"

- The coupon discount is **distributed proportionally** across all items in the order.
- If you return some but not all items, the coupon is **not refunded** — you already received the benefit.
- If the entire order is cancelled before shipping, the coupon is **returned** to your account.

## Other Coupon Issues

### "Coupon not sent after promotion"

- Some promotions have a **delivery delay** of up to 24 hours.
- Check **My Vouchers** — they may be categorized as "Unused."
- Contact support with the promotion name if not received after 24 hours.

### "I accidentally claimed a coupon I don't need"

- Unused coupons **cannot be transferred** to other users.
- They will expire on their own after the validity period.
- You can **ignore** the coupon — unused coupons don't affect your account.

### "How do I get more coupons?"

| Method | Details |
|:-------|:--------|
| Daily Check-in | Log in daily to earn check-in coupons. |
| Flash Sale events | Special coupons during Flash Sale periods. |
| Referral program | Invite friends to earn referral coupons. |
| Birthday voucher | Automatic on your birthday (Gold tier and above). |
| Promotional emails | Subscribe to marketing emails for exclusive codes. |

## Still Having Coupon Issues?

If your coupon issue is not listed here, contact support with:
1. The **coupon code** (or screenshot of My Vouchers).
2. Your **order total** before and after applying the coupon.
3. **Screenshots** of any error messages.
