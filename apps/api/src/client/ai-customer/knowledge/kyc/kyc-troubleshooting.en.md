# KYC Troubleshooting

## Common Rejection Reasons

### "Photo is blurry or unclear"

**Why**: The uploaded photo of your ID or selfie is not clear enough for verification.

**Solution**:
1. Ensure you are in a **well-lit area** (natural daylight is best).
2. Hold the camera **steady** — avoid shaky hands.
3. Make sure the **entire ID** is visible in the frame (no cropping).
4. Ensure **no glare** or reflection on the ID.
5. Take the photo against a **plain background** (white wall).

### "ID document is expired"

**Why**: The government ID you submitted has passed its expiration date.

**Solution**:
1. Check the expiry date on your ID.
2. Submit a different, valid ID from the accepted list.
3. If you've renewed your ID, use the renewed version.

Accepted IDs: Passport, UMID, Driver's License, PRC ID, Postal ID, Senior Citizen ID, PhilSys ID. See [KYC Guide](kyc-guide.en.md).

### "Name mismatch"

**Why**: The name on your submitted ID does not match your JoyMini account name.

**Solution**:
1. Check the exact spelling of your name on your ID.
2. Update your profile name in **Account → Settings** to match the ID.
3. If your ID has a middle name, ensure your profile includes it.
4. If married and your surname changed, submit a Marriage Certificate along with your ID.

### "Face does not match ID photo"

**Why**: The selfie you took does not match the photo on your government ID.

**Solution**:
1. Remove any accessories that may alter your appearance (sunglasses, masks, heavy makeup).
2. Ensure the lighting is even on your face.
3. Make sure you are facing the camera directly.
4. If you've changed significantly since your ID was issued, use a more recent ID.

### "Document is not an accepted ID type"

**Why**: The document you submitted is not on our list of accepted government IDs.

**Accepted IDs**:
- Passport
- UMID (Unified Multi-Purpose ID)
- Driver's License
- PRC ID
- Postal ID
- Senior Citizen ID
- PhilSys ID (Philippine National ID)

**Company IDs, school IDs, and barangay certificates are NOT accepted.**

### "Incomplete document — missing back side"

**Why**: Some ID types require both front and back photos.

**Solution**:
1. Check if your ID type requires both sides (Driver's License and UMID require both).
2. Take clear photos of both the front and back of your ID.
3. Ensure all text and barcodes on the back are visible.

## Document Upload Issues

### "Upload failed"

| Cause | Solution |
|:------|:---------|
| File too large | Compress the image to under 5MB. |
| Unsupported format | Use JPG, PNG, or PDF format only. |
| Network error | Check your internet connection and retry. |
| Server timeout | Wait a few minutes and try again. |

### "Camera not working"

- Grant camera permissions in your phone settings.
- If using the app, ensure it has camera access.
- Try uploading a photo from your gallery instead.

## Processing Time Issues

### "My KYC has been pending for a long time"

| Status | Expected Time | What to Do |
|:-------|:-------------:|:-----------|
| Under review | 24 hours | Wait. Check back after 24 hours. |
| Pending > 48 hours | — | Contact support with your KYC submission ID. |
| Re-reviewed | 24 hours after resubmission | Wait for the new review. |

### "Why is my KYC taking longer than usual?"

Possible reasons:
- High volume of KYC applications (new promotion period).
- Your document requires manual review (e.g., uncommon ID type).
- Technical issue with the verification system.

## Still Can't Pass KYC?

If you've tried everything and still can't complete verification:

1. Contact support via in-app chat.
2. Provide:
   - A clear photo of your valid government ID
   - A selfie holding the ID next to your face
   - Your User ID
3. Our team will manually verify your identity.

See [KYC Guide](kyc-guide.en.md) for the full KYC process, or [Contact Support](../common/contact-support.en.md).
