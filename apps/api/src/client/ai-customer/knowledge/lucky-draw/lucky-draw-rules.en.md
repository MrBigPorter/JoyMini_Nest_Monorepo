# Lucky Draw Rules

## How Lucky Draw Works

1. Browse **Flash Sale** items in the app.
2. Each item has a **ticket price** (not the full retail price).
3. Purchase **tickets** for the item you want.
4. Each ticket gives you **one entry** into the draw.
5. The winner is randomly selected when the draw closes.
6. Non-winners get their ticket price refunded as **wallet credit**.

## Eligibility

- Must have a **verified account** (KYC Level 1 minimum).
- Must be **18 years or older**.
- One account per person. Multiple accounts for lucky draw participation is prohibited.
- Valid payment method required (GCash, PayMaya, or wallet balance).

## Draw Process

1. **Ticket Sales Period**: Usually 24-72 hours.
2. **Draw**: Conducted within 1 hour after sales close.
3. **Winner Notification**: Via SMS + in-app notification.
4. **Prize Claim**: Winner must claim within **48 hours**.
5. **Unclaimed Prizes**: If not claimed, a new draw is held.

## Prize Information

- **Prize type**: Physical item, digital code, or wallet credit.
- **Delivery**: Physical items shipped within **5 business days**.
- **Digital prizes**: Delivered instantly via in-app notification.
- **Tax**: Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.

## Refunds for Non-Winners

- Refund is **automatic** — no need to request.
- Refund goes to **wallet balance**, usable for any purchase.
- Processing time: Within **1 hour** after draw results.

## Rules & Restrictions

- JoyMini employees and their relatives are **not eligible** to participate.
- Suspicious activity (e.g., bot participation) will result in disqualification.
- JoyMini reserves the right to cancel or modify any draw with prior notice.
- Maximum ticket purchase per user per draw: **100 tickets**.
