# COD (Cash on Delivery) Payment Guide

## What is COD?

Cash on Delivery (COD) is a payment method where you **pay in cash** when your order is delivered to your door. This is one of the most popular payment methods in the Philippines.

## Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
|:------------|:--------|
| **Order total** | ₱100 — ₱10,000 |
| **Available in** | All Philippine provinces (except remote islands) |
| **Account** | Any account with completed KYC Level 1 |
| **Item type** | Most physical items (excluding digital goods, GCash pins) |

## How COD Works

1. Place an order and select **Cash on Delivery** as payment method.
2. No upfront payment needed.
3. The order is processed and shipped as usual.
4. When the courier arrives at your door, **pay the exact amount in cash**.
5. The courier will hand over your package after payment.

## COD Fees

| Order Amount | COD Fee |
|:------------|:--------|
| ₱100 — ₱500 | ₱20 |
| ₱500 — ₱1,000 | ₱30 |
| ₱1,000 — ₱3,000 | ₱50 |
| ₱3,000 — ₱5,000 | ₱70 |
| ₱5,000 — ₱10,000 | ₱100 |

The COD fee is added to your order total at checkout.

## What If I'm Not Home?

The courier will attempt delivery **3 times**:

| Attempt | Action |
|:--------|:-------|
| **1st attempt** | Courier calls you. If unavailable, will try next day. |
| **2nd attempt** | Courier calls again. If unavailable, SMS notification sent. |
| **3rd attempt** | Final attempt. If still unavailable, the order is cancelled. |

### Important
- Most couriers call **30 minutes before arrival**.
- You can reschedule by contacting the courier directly via the tracking number.
- After 3 failed attempts, the order is **automatically cancelled** and no refund is needed (no payment was made).

## Can I Change My Mind?

| Scenario | What Happens |
|:---------|:-------------|
| I want to cancel before delivery | Free cancellation. Go to **My Orders** → Cancel Order. |
| I refuse the package at delivery | Order is returned. No charge. |
| Courier arrives but I have insufficient cash | Partial payment is **not accepted**. Reschedule delivery. |

## Important Notes

- **Prepare exact change** — couriers may not have change for large bills.
- **Inspect the package** before paying — open the box and check the item.
- **Damaged items**: Do NOT pay. Refuse delivery and report to support.
- COD cannot be combined with **wallet payment** or **voucher discounts** that require online payment.

## COD vs Other Payment Methods

| Feature | COD | GCash | Card |
|:--------|:---:|:-----:|:----:|
| Pay at delivery | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

| Can use vouchers | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cashback eligible | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Return shipping covered | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
