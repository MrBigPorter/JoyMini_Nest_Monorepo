# Refund Policy

## Return Conditions

- Items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Items must be **unused** and in **original packaging**.
- Certain items (e.g., digital goods, perishables) are **not eligible** for return.
- Promotional or "Flash Sale" items may have **different return policies**. Check the product page for details.

## Refund Process

1. Go to **My Orders** page.
2. Select the order and click **Request Return/Refund**.
3. Fill in the reason and upload photos if needed.
4. Submit the request. Our team will review within **24-48 hours**.
5. Once approved, you will receive a return shipping label.
6. Ship the item back. Refund will be processed **within 5 business days** after we receive the item.

## Refund Amount

| Condition | Refund Amount |
|:----------|:--------------|
| Item not received | 100% refund |
| Item defective/damaged | 100% refund + return shipping covered |
| Changed mind | 100% refund (buyer pays return shipping) |
| Late delivery (>7 days) | 100% refund + 10% compensation coupon |

## Refund Methods

| Original Payment Method | Refund Method | Processing Time |
|:------------------------|:--------------|:----------------|
| GCash / PayMaya | Wallet credit | 1-3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | Bank account | 3-7 business days |
| Credit/Debit Card | Card reversal | 5-10 business days |
| COD (Cash on Delivery) | Wallet credit | 1-3 business days |

## Timeline

- **Review**: 24-48 hours after submission
- **Return shipping**: 3-7 days (depending on location)
- **Inspection**: 1-2 business days after receipt
- **Refund**: 5 business days after inspection approval

## Exceptions

- **Group buying orders**: Refund processed only after group fails. See [Group Buying Guide](../product/group-buying-guide.en.md).
- **Lucky draw items**: Cannot be returned. Damaged items may be replaced.
- **Digital/pin codes**: Non-refundable once delivered.
