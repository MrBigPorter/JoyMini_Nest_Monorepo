# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Timeline

| Order Type | Processing Time | Shipping Time (Metro Manila) | Shipping Time (Provincial) |
|:-----------|:----------------|:----------------------------|:---------------------------|
| Standard | 1-2 business days | 2-4 business days | 5-8 business days |
| Express | Same day if ordered before 2PM | 1-2 business days | 2-4 business days |
| Flash Sale | 2-3 business days | 3-5 business days | 5-10 business days |
| Pre-order | As stated on product page | Varies | Varies |

## Shipping Fees

| Order Amount | Standard Shipping | Express Shipping |
|:------------|:-----------------|:-----------------|
| Below ₱500 | ₱50 | ₱120 |
| ₱500 - ₱999 | Free | ₱80 |
| ₱1,000+ | Free | Free |

## Shipping Partners

- **J&T Express** — Standard delivery (nationwide)
- **LBC** — Provincial areas, remote locations
- **Ninja Van** — Metro Manila express delivery
- **2GO** — Palawan, Visayas, Mindanao remote areas

## Delivery Address

- You can change the delivery address **before the order is shipped**.
- To change: Go to **My Orders** → Select order → **Edit Address**.
- Once shipped, address cannot be changed. Contact the courier directly.

## Tracking

- Tracking number will be sent via **SMS** and **email** once shipped.
- You can also track in **My Orders** page.
- If no tracking update for **7 days**, contact our support team.

## Failed Delivery

- If delivery fails after **3 attempts**, the package will be returned to sender.
- You will be refunded (minus original shipping fee) after we receive the returned package.
- Re-delivery fee: ₱50.
