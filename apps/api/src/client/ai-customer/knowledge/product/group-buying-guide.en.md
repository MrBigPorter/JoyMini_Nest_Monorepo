# Group Buying Guide

## What is Group Buying?

Group buying allows you to get a **lower price** by purchasing together with other users. When enough people join the same purchase, everyone gets the discount.

## Pricing Tiers

| Price Type | Description |
|:-----------|:------------|
| **Unit Price** (Group Price) | Lowest price — requires group to be formed |
| **Solo Price** | Higher price — buy immediately without waiting for a group |
| **Market Price** | Original retail price shown for comparison |

## How It Works

1. Find a product with group pricing.
2. Choose to **Start a Group** or **Join an Existing Group**.
3. Pay the **unit price** (group price) or **solo price** (instant buy).
4. If you started a group, share the invite link with friends.
5. When the group reaches the required size, the purchase is confirmed at the group price.
6. If the group doesn't fill within the time limit, the order is **cancelled and refunded**.

## Group Buying Rules

| Rule | Details |
|:-----|:--------|
| Group Size | Varies by product (typically 2-5 people) |
| Time Limit | Usually 24-72 hours (shown on product page) |
| Minimum Order | Each person must purchase at least 1 item |
| Cancellation | You can cancel **before** the group is formed |
| Refund | Automatic if group fails — no action needed |

## Tips for Success

1. **Share immediately** after starting a group — time is limited.
2. Share on social media (Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp).
3. Invite friends who are also interested in the product.
4. The more people in your group, the better the price.

## Group vs Solo Purchase

| Aspect | Group Purchase | Solo Purchase |
|:-------|:--------------|:--------------|
| Price | Lower (unit price) | Higher (solo price) |
| Wait time | May need to wait for group to fill | Instant |
| Risk | Group may fail = cancellation | No risk |
| Best for | Not urgent, want best price | Urgent, need item now |
