# Product Authenticity

## Our Authenticity Guarantee

All products sold on JoyMini are **100% authentic and genuine**. We work directly with brand distributors, authorized dealers, and manufacturers to ensure every item is authentic.

## How We Ensure Authenticity

| Measure | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Direct sourcing** | Products sourced directly from brands or authorized distributors |
| **Supplier verification** | All suppliers undergo strict verification (business permits, distribution rights) |
| **Batch inspection** | Random sampling inspection for every batch of products |
| **Serial number tracking** | High-value items tracked via serial numbers |
| **Anti-tamper seals** | Products sealed with JoyMini authenticity stickers |

## How to Verify Authenticity

### Method 1: QR Code Scan
1. Look for the **JoyMini Authenticity QR code** on the product packaging.
2. Scan with your phone camera or the JoyMini app.
3. The verification result will show: **"Authentic Product"** with product details.

### Method 2: Serial Number Check
1. Find the serial number on the product or packaging.
2. Go to **Account → Verify Product**.
3. Enter the serial number to check authenticity.

### Method 3: Contact Support
If you have doubts about any product, contact support with:
- Order number
- Product photos (packaging, labels, serial number)
- Receipt

See [Contact Support](../common/contact-support.en.md).

## What to Do If You Suspect a Fake

If you believe you received a counterfeit product:

1. **Do not use or dispose of the product.**
2. Take clear photos of:
   - The product
   - Packaging (front and back)
   - Any labels or serial numbers
   - The delivery receipt
3. Contact support **within 48 hours of delivery**.
4. We will investigate and, if confirmed counterfeit:
   - **Full refund** including shipping
   - **₱500 compensation** for the inconvenience
   - Report the seller to authorities

## Seller Authenticity Policy

| Rule | Details |
|:-----|:--------|
| **Selling fakes** | First offense: permanent account suspension, legal action |
| **Mislabelling** | Selling used items as new: account suspension |
| **Unauthorized goods** | Selling without distribution rights: delisting + warning |
| **Repeat violations** | Automatic permanent ban |

## Tips for Buyers

- **Check seller ratings** before purchasing from new sellers.
- **Verified badges** indicate the seller has passed our verification process.
- **Price too good to be true?** Compare with official brand prices. Extremely low prices may indicate counterfeit goods.
- **Report suspicious listings** — tap the "Report" button on any product page.
