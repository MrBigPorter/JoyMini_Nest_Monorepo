# Product FAQ

## Stock & Availability

### "Is this product in stock?"

Stock status is shown on the **product detail page**:
- **In Stock** — Available for immediate purchase.
- **Low Stock** — Few units remaining. Order soon.
- **Out of Stock** — Currently unavailable.
- **Pre-Sale** — Not yet available. Can place an order for future delivery.

You can also use `getProductStatus` to check real-time stock information for any product.

### "Will you restock this item?"

| Scenario | What Happens |
|:---------|:-------------|
| Popular items | Most popular items are restocked regularly. Check back in 1-2 weeks. |
| Limited edition | Usually **not restocked**. Once sold out, they're gone. |
| Seasonal items | Restocked next season. |
| Flash Sale items | Restocked per Flash Sale schedule. |

**To get notified when an item is back in stock:**
1. Go to the product page.
2. Tap **"Notify Me When Available"** (bell icon).
3. You'll receive an **in-app notification** when the item is restocked.

### "How long does restocking take?"

Typical restocking time: **1–4 weeks**, depending on the supplier and product type.

- **Local sellers**: 1–2 weeks.
- **International suppliers**: 2–4 weeks (includes shipping time).
- **Pre-order items**: The estimated availability date is shown on the product page.

### "Can I pre-order an out-of-stock item?"

If the product has a **"Pre-Sale"** label, yes! Pre-sale items can be ordered before they arrive in our warehouse.

1. Add the item to your cart (same process as a regular order).
2. Proceed to checkout.
3. The estimated delivery date will be displayed.
4. Your payment will be processed **at the time of order**.
5. The item will ship once it arrives in our warehouse.

See [Order Management](../order/order-management.en.md) for order status tracking.

### "Can I backorder an item?"

Not all items support backordering. If a product page shows **"Out of Stock"** without a Pre-Sale option, you cannot place an order. Tap **"Notify Me When Available"** instead.

## Product Variants

### "Can I choose a different color/size?"

If the product has **variants** (color, size, etc.), they will be shown on the product page as selectable options. Simply select your preferred variant before adding to cart.

If no variant options are shown, the product is available in **one configuration only**.

### "My preferred variant is out of stock, but other variants are available"

Unfortunately, you can only purchase the variants that are **in stock**. We cannot hold or reserve out-of-stock variants.

- You can purchase the in-stock variant now.
- Check back later — different variants may be restocked at different times.
- Tap **"Notify Me"** on your preferred variant.

### "Can I request a variant that isn't listed?"

We cannot accommodate custom variant requests. Product variants are determined by the supplier/manufacturer. You can:
1. Check other products in the same category for similar items.
2. Contact support to suggest the variant to our product team.

## Warranty & Quality

### "Do products come with a warranty?"

| Product Type | Warranty |
|:-------------|:---------|
| **Electronics** | 6 months to 1 year brand warranty |
| **Appliances** | 6 months to 1 year brand warranty |
| **Fashion & Accessories** | No warranty (but returnable — see Refund Policy) |
| **Beauty & Personal Care** | No warranty |
| **Flash Sale / Lucky Draw items** | No warranty (final sale) |

Warranty details are shown on each **product page**. If not specified, assume no warranty.

### "How do I claim warranty service?"

1. Contact the **brand's service center** directly (warranty card included in packaging).
2. If you need assistance, contact JoyMini support with:
   - Order number
   - Product photos showing the issue
   - Description of the problem
3. Our team will help facilitate the warranty claim.

### "What if the product arrives damaged?"

1. **Do not use or dispose of the product.**
2. Take clear photos of the damage **within 48 hours of delivery**.
3. Contact support via **Order → Report Issue**.
4. We will arrange a **return and replacement or refund**.

See [Refund Policy](../order/refund-policy.en.md) for details.

### "Can I return a product I don't like?"

Yes, most products can be returned within **7 days of delivery** if you're not satisfied. Conditions apply:

- Item must be **unused, unwashed, and in original packaging**.
- Some items are non-returnable (underwear, swimwear, perishable goods, Flash Sale items).
- Return shipping may apply.

See the full [Refund Policy](../order/refund-policy.en.md) for details.

## Product Information

### "How do I find the right product for me?"

| If you want... | Try this |
|:---------------|:---------|
| Specific product | Use **search** with the product name or keyword. |
| Browse categories | Go to **Categories** and explore by department. |
| Best sellers | Check **Hot Products** for trending items. |
| Best value | Sort by **Price: Low to High** or look for **Flash Sale** items. |
| New arrivals | Sort by **Newest** on category pages. |
| Recommendations | Check your **Recommended for You** section on the home page. |

### "How do I know if a product is good quality?"

1. **Check reviews and ratings** — other buyers' experiences are the best indicator.
2. **Check seller rating** — verified sellers with high ratings are more reliable.
3. **Look for verified badges** — products from authorized distributors are marked.
4. **Check product photos** — detailed photos from multiple angles indicate confidence in the product.
5. **Read the description carefully** — materials, dimensions, and features are listed.

### "Are the product photos accurate?"

We require sellers to provide **actual product photos**. However:
- Colors may vary slightly due to **monitor settings**.
- For fashion items, **model photos** may show the item styled differently.
- Check **customer review photos** for real-life images.

### "What if the product I received looks different from the photos?"

1. Document the difference with photos.
2. Contact support within **48 hours** of delivery.
3. If the product significantly differs from the listing, you are entitled to a **full refund**.

### "I'm not sure which size to order"

See our detailed [Size Guide](size-guide.en.md) for measurements and size conversion charts.

### "Are refurbished or second-hand items sold?"

JoyMini does **not** sell refurbished or second-hand items unless explicitly stated on the product page. All products are brand new and authentic. See [Product Authenticity](product-authenticity.en.md) for our authenticity guarantee.

## Flash Sale & Lucky Draw

### "What's the difference between a regular product and a Flash Sale product?"

| Feature | Regular Product | Flash Sale Product |
|:--------|:---------------:|:------------------:|
| Price | Standard pricing | Discounted price |
| Stock | Usually available | Limited stock, time-limited |
| Warranty | Yes (for electronics/appliances) | No (final sale) |
| Returns | 7-day return policy | No returns (unless damaged) |
| Coupons can be applied | Yes | No |

See [Lucky Draw Rules](../lucky-draw/lucky-draw-rules.en.md) for Lucky Draw product rules.

### "Can I use a coupon on Flash Sale items?"

No, Flash Sale and Lucky Draw items are **not eligible** for coupon discounts. See [Coupon Rules](../coupon/coupon-rules.en.md) for details.

## Pricing

### "Will the price change after I add to cart?"

The price at **checkout** is the final price. If the product's price changes while in your cart, the current price will be applied at checkout. Prices may change due to:
- Flash Sale ending.
- Promotion period ending.
- Price adjustment by the seller.

### "Do you offer price matching?"

JoyMini does **not** offer price matching. We recommend:
1. Checking multiple sellers for the same product.
2. Waiting for Flash Sale events for the best prices.
3. Using available coupons and vouchers at checkout.
