# Reviews & Ratings Guide

## How to Leave a Review

You can leave a review for any product you've purchased.

### From Your Orders

1. Go to **My Orders** and find the completed order.
2. Tap **"Write a Review"** next to the product.
3. Rate the product (1-5 stars).
4. Write your review (minimum 10 characters).
5. Optionally, add **photos** (up to 5 images).
6. Tap **Submit**.

### From Product Page

1. Go to the product page.
2. Scroll down to the **Reviews** section.
3. Tap **"Write a Review"**.
4. Rate and write your review.
5. Submit.

### Review Guidelines

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|:-----|:---------|
| Share your honest experience | Post fake or misleading reviews |
| Include helpful photos of the product | Include personal contact information |
| Describe product quality, fit, and features | Use profanity or offensive language |
| Mention shipping experience (if relevant) | Post about unrelated products |
| Be specific about what you liked/disliked | Include links to external websites |

## Review Timeline

| Event | When |
|:------|:-----|
| Review window opens | After order is marked as **Delivered** |
| Review window closes | **30 days** after delivery |
| Review posted | Immediately after submission (may have a short moderation delay) |
| Review visible | On the product page's Reviews section |

## Photo & Video Reviews

Photo and video reviews are highly encouraged!

- **Photos help** other buyers see the actual product.
- You can upload up to **5 photos** per review.
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG (max 5MB each).
- Videos are not currently supported in reviews.

**Pro tip**: Reviews with photos get **more helpful votes** and help other customers make better buying decisions.

## Editing or Deleting a Review

### Can I edit my review?

Yes, within **7 days** of posting:
1. Go to **My Account → My Reviews**.
2. Find the review and tap **Edit**.
3. Update your rating, text, or photos.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Can I delete my review?

Yes, within **7 days** of posting:
1. Go to **My Account → My Reviews**.
2. Find the review and tap **Delete**.
3. Confirm deletion.

### After 7 days

Reviews become **locked** after 7 days and cannot be edited or deleted. If you have a valid reason to change a locked review, contact support.

## Seller Responses

Sellers can **respond** to reviews publicly. You will receive a notification if the seller replies to your review. The seller's response will appear below your review on the product page.

## Review Moderation

### Why was my review rejected?

| Reason | Solution |
|:-------|:---------|
| Contains profanity or offensive language | Remove offensive content and resubmit |
| Includes personal information (phone, address) | Remove personal info and resubmit |
| Spam or irrelevant content | Write a review about the product itself |
| Suspected fake review | Provide proof of purchase if requested |
| Violates platform guidelines | Review our Review Guidelines above |

### Reporting a Review

If you see a review that violates our guidelines:
1. Tap the **flag icon** next to the review.
2. Select the reason for reporting.
3. Submit. Our team will review within **24 hours**.

## Ratings & Product Quality

### How is the average rating calculated?

The average rating is calculated as the **mean of all ratings** for the product. For example:
- 10 five-star ratings + 5 four-star ratings = (10×5 + 5×4) ÷ 15 = **4.67 stars**

### Why are there no reviews for a new product?

New products may not have reviews yet. Check:
- **Other products** from the same seller.
- The seller's **overall rating** on their store page.
- **Product descriptions and photos** for quality indicators.

### "There are good and bad reviews — should I buy?"

Look for **patterns** in reviews:
- **Consistent complaints** about the same issue (e.g., sizing, durability) → potential quality issue.
- **One-off bad reviews** among mostly positive ones → may be isolated incidents.
- **Verified purchase** reviews are more reliable than unverified ones.
- Check **review photos** to see the actual product.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Can I review without purchasing?"
No, only customers who have purchased the product can leave a review.

### "Can I review a product I bought as a gift?"
Yes, if the order is linked to your account.

### "Do you verify purchases?"
Yes, reviews from verified purchases are marked with a **"Verified Purchase"** badge.

### "Can sellers remove negative reviews?"
No. Sellers cannot remove or hide reviews. JoyMini moderates reviews fairly.

### "What if my review was removed unfairly?"
Contact support with your review details and we will investigate.
