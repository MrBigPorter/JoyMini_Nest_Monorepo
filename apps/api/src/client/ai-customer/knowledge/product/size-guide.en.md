## How to Measure Yourself

| Measurement | How to Measure |
|:------------|:---------------|
| **Bust/Chest** | Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal. |
| **Waist** | Measure around the narrowest part of your waist (usually above the belly button). |
| **Hips** | Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the tape horizontal. |
| **Shoulder** | Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other. |
| **Inseam** | Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg. |
| **Sleeve** | Measure from the shoulder seam to the wrist bone. |

### Tips for Accurate Measurement

- Use a **flexible measuring tape** (not a metal ruler).
- Wear **light clothing** or measure against your skin.
- Keep the tape **snug but not tight** — you should be able to insert one finger.
- **Stand straight** with your arms relaxed at your sides.
- Ask someone to help for more accurate measurements.

## Size Selection Tips

### Asian vs Western Sizing
- Asian/Philippine sizes tend to run **smaller** than US/EU sizes.
- If you usually wear **US size M**, we recommend **Asian size L**.
- Check the product's **specific size chart** — each brand may vary.

### For Specific Body Types
| If you are... | Recommendation |
|:--------------|:---------------|
| Tall (170cm+ for women, 180cm+ for men) | Size up for longer sleeve/inseam |
| Petite (under 155cm) | Size down or look for "Petite" labeled items |
| Plus size (US 18+) | Look for "Plus Size" category |
| Muscular/broad shoulders | Size up for better shoulder fit |

## Still Not Sure About Size?

- Check **product reviews** — other buyers often mention if the item runs small or large.
- Look for **model measurements** in product images (height, weight, size worn).
- If between sizes, we recommend **sizing up** — it's easier to alter a slightly larger item.
- Contact support for brand-specific size advice.
