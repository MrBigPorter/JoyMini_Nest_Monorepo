# VIP Benefits & Membership

| Tier | Requirements | Duration |
|:-----|:-------------|:---------|

## Promotion & Demotion Rules

### Upgrade
- Tier upgrades are **automatic** when requirements are met.
- Benefits take effect **immediately** after upgrade.
- Upgrade evaluation runs **daily**.

### Renewal
- Silver/Gold tiers expire after the duration period.
- You must meet the requirements again during the evaluation window to renew.
- If requirements are **not met**, you are downgraded one tier.

### Downgrade
- **Gold → Silver**: If spending falls below threshold during evaluation period.
- **Silver → Bronze**: If fewer than 5 orders in the evaluation period.
- **Diamond/Platinum**: Invitation-only tiers are reviewed quarterly.

## How to Check Your Tier

1. Go to **Account → My VIP**.
2. Your current tier, progress to next tier, and remaining duration are displayed.
3. Progress bar shows: orders completed / orders needed, or amount spent / amount needed.
