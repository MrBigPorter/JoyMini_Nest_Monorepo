# Deposit Guide

## Supported Deposit Methods

| Method | Processing Time | Min Amount | Max Amount | Fee |
|:-------|:---------------:|:----------:|:----------:|:---:|
| **GCash** | Instant | ₱50 | ₱50,000 | Free |
| **PayMaya / Maya** | Instant | ₱50 | ₱50,000 | Free |
| **Bank Transfer (InstaPay)** | Instant | ₱100 | ₱50,000 | Free |
| **Bank Transfer (PESONet)** | 1-3 hours | ₱100 | ₱100,000 | Free |
| **Credit/Debit Card** | Instant | ₱100 | ₱25,000 | 2.5% fee |
| **Over-the-Counter** | 1-24 hours | ₱100 | ₱10,000 | ₱10 fee |

## How to Deposit via GCash

1. Open the JoyMini app and go to **Wallet → Deposit**.
2. Select **GCash** as the payment method.
3. Enter the amount you wish to deposit (₱50 minimum).
4. You will be redirected to the GCash app.
5. Confirm the payment in GCash.
6. Return to JoyMini. Your wallet will be credited **instantly**.

## How to Deposit via Bank Transfer (InstaPay)

1. Go to **Wallet → Deposit** and select **Bank Transfer (InstaPay)**.
2. Copy the virtual account number displayed.
3. Open your banking app and add JoyMini as a payee.
4. Transfer the amount using the virtual account number.
5. The funds will be credited **within seconds**.

## How to Deposit via Over-the-Counter

1. Go to **Wallet → Deposit** and select **Over-the-Counter**.
2. You will receive a payment reference code.
3. Visit any **7-Eleven, MLhuillier, or Palawan Pawnshop** branch.
4. Provide the reference code and pay the amount in cash.
5. Funds will be credited within **1-24 hours**.

## Deposit Not Credited?

If your deposit was deducted from your bank/GCash but not reflected in your JoyMini wallet:

1. **Wait 30 minutes** — most delays resolve automatically.
2. **Check transaction history** in Wallet → Transactions.
3. If still not credited after 30 min, contact support with:
   - Screenshot of the bank/GCash receipt
   - Deposit amount and date
   - Payment method used

See [Wallet Troubleshooting](wallet-troubleshooting.en.md) for more help.

Higher deposit limits require completing a higher KYC level. See [KYC Guide](../kyc/kyc-guide.en.md).

## Important Notes

- Deposits are **non-reversible** once completed.
- Always double-check the account number/GCash number before confirming.
- JoyMini will **never** ask for your GCash/ bank PIN or OTP outside the app.
- For suspicious activity, report immediately via [Contact Support](../common/contact-support.en.md).
